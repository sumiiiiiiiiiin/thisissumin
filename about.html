Hi! I’m Sumin!

From chemical engineering to Interaction Design, I’ve always been curious about how things work and how to make them better.

Today, I bring together design, code, and AI to turn ideas into experiences.

My journey started in engineering, where I learned to approach problems step by step. But I soon realized I was more interested in the creative side of problem-solving, which led me to work at a web agency creating digital magazines and event pages.

That experience sparked my curiosity about design. Eventually, I packed my bags and moved to Canada to study Interaction Design at George Brown College, where I explored UX, UI, interactive media, and front-end development.

Today, I use AI to bridge the gap between design and development, turning my ideas into working products and interactive experiences.

When I’m not designing, you’ll probably find me thrifting, traveling, or playing guitar. I’ve also spent 18 months traveling with just a backpack! So yes, I’m always ready for the next adventure.

Let’s grab a coffee and make something!
